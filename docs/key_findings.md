## Finding 1 — Beverages is the Revenue Engine

Beverages accounts for approximately **33.8% of all transactions** (169 out of 500) and the highest revenue contribution (₹1,17,511.08 — 48.1% of total sales) among all six categories. The category's strength comes from an unusual combination of premium items (Cheese at ₹185.50/unit, Coffee at ₹140) alongside everyday necessities (Milk at ₹95, Tea at ₹75). This dual positioning ensures consistent demand across all customer segments, income levels, and purchase occasions.

---

## Finding 2 — Cheese Drives Disproportionate Revenue

Cheese (₹185.50 per unit) is the single most frequently purchased product by sales revenue (₹58,066.08 — 23.8% of total revenue), appearing in 55 transactions with a total of 313 units sold. Its combination of high unit price and high purchase frequency makes it the most important product in the catalogue for revenue protection.

---

## Finding 3 — Three Branches Perform Near-Equally

Branch A (Delhi), Branch B (Bangalore), and Branch C (Mumbai) each contribute approximately one-third of total annual revenue. Branch C (Mumbai) holds a marginal lead with ₹87,956.58 (36.0%), versus Branch B (Bangalore) at ₹78,555 (32.1%) and Branch A (Delhi) at ₹77,899.50 (31.9%). This balance is a positive operational signal — the business is not over-dependent on any single location.

---

## Finding 4 — UPI Dominates Payments; 70.4% of Transactions are Cashless

UPI is used in **194 transactions (38.8%)**, making it the single most-used payment method. When combined with Credit Card (158 transactions, 31.6%), **70.4% of all transactions are cashless**. This reflects the high digital payment adoption across Mumbai, Bangalore, and Delhi. Cash accounts for 148 transactions (29.6%).

---

## Finding 5 — Customer Type Performance is Closely Matched

Member customers (264 transactions, 52.8%) generate total sales of ₹1,27,596.08, averaging ₹483.32 per transaction. Normal customers (236 transactions, 47.2%) generate ₹1,16,815.00, averaging ₹495.00 per transaction. Normal customers show a slightly higher average transaction value. Members show a marginally higher rating (avg 3.978) versus Normal (avg 4.003 — Normal is marginally higher). The 47.2% Normal customer base remains a significant conversion opportunity for growing Member share.

## Finding 7 — Customer Satisfaction is Consistent but Below Excellent

An average rating of **3.99/5** indicates that customers are generally satisfied but not delighted. No transaction received a rating below 3.5, which establishes a healthy minimum floor. Approximately 17% of transactions achieved an "excellent" rating (4.5+). Dairy is the highest-rated category (avg 4.018); Grocery is the lowest (avg 3.947). Branch B (Bangalore) leads on average rating (4.104), followed by Branch A (Delhi, 4.059) and Branch C (Mumbai, 3.825).

---

## Finding 8 — Rice and Tea Are the High-Volume Products

While Cheese leads in total sales revenue, **Tea (62 transactions, 359 units)** and **Rice (58 transactions, 348 units)** are the two highest-frequency products by transaction count. Their lower unit prices (₹75 and ₹80 respectively) create broad customer engagement. Combined, Tea and Rice account for 120 transactions — 24% of all transactions — making them critical foot-traffic products despite lower per-unit revenue.

---

## Finding 9 — Bakery and Grocery Are Underdeveloped Single-Product Categories

Bakery (Bread, ₹55/unit, 40 transactions, ₹11,000 revenue — 4.5% of total) and Grocery (Sugar, ₹50/unit, 34 transactions, ₹8,500 revenue — 3.5% of total) are the two smallest categories by both transaction count and revenue. Both are currently single-product categories, representing immediate expansion opportunities. Adding 2–3 products to each category could meaningfully grow their combined share.

---

## Finding 10 — 500 Transactions Across 12 Months is a Strong Analytics Foundation

With exactly 500 transactions spread across all 12 months of 2024 (January 2024 – December 2024), 3 branches, 3 cities, 15 products, 6 categories, 3 payment methods, and 2 customer types, the dataset provides **sufficient coverage for all core business intelligence dimensions**. The analysis is grounded in complete, clean, verified data with no missing values and no duplicates.
