## 2. Top Product — Cheese

| Metric | Value |

## 4. Observations

1. **Cheese (₹185.50)** is consistently the top product because it combines high unit price with high purchase frequency. Customers regularly buy 3–6 units per visit.

2. **Paneer (₹200.00)** has the highest unit price but slightly lower transaction frequency than Cheese. Individual transactions involving Paneer generate very high revenue per invoice.

3. **Eggs** appear frequently at 6 units per transaction. Despite a lower unit price (₹65), volume drives their revenue contribution.

4. **Snack products** (Biscuits, Chips) are high in frequency but low in per-transaction value — they function as basket add-ons rather than revenue anchors.

5. **Bread** and **Sugar** are the lowest revenue contributors but serve an essential role in driving foot traffic to the store.

---

## 6. Business Implication

- **Protect and promote Cheese and Paneer**: These two products drive disproportionate revenue per transaction. Stockout of either would materially impact daily revenue.
- **Bundle budget snacks with premium items**: Pairing Chips/Biscuits with Cheese or Coffee as combo offers could increase average basket size.
- **Eggs are a high-volume driver**: Ensure consistent egg supply, especially at Branch C where multi-unit purchases are most frequent.
