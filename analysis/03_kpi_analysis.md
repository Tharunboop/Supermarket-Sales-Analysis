## KPI Detail

### 1. Total Sales — ₹2,44,411.08

- Total revenue generated across all 500 transactions in 2024.
- Calculated as the sum of `Sales` column across the entire dataset.
- Includes all branches, cities, and product categories.

### 2. Total Transactions — 500

- Each row in the dataset represents a unique transaction identified by a unique Invoice ID.
- 500 transactions spread across January to December 2024.
- Approximately 41–42 transactions per month on average.

### 3. Average Transaction Value (ATV) — ₹488.82

- **Formula:** Total Sales ÷ Total Transactions = ₹2,44,411.08 ÷ 500
- Represents the average revenue generated per customer visit.
- Benchmark metric for sales performance and basket size monitoring.

### 4. Average Customer Rating — 3.99 / 5

- Calculated as the mean of the `Rating` column across all 500 transactions.
- Ratings range from 3.5 (minimum) to 4.8 (maximum).
- A rating of ~4.0 indicates moderate-to-good customer satisfaction.
- No single branch or product drives significantly above or below the mean.

### 5. Total Quantity Sold — 2,768 Units

- Sum of the `Quantity` column across all transactions.
- Average of 5.54 units per transaction.
- Beverages account for the largest share of quantity sold.

### 6. Top Product — Cheese

- Cheese (Unit Price: ₹185.50) appears most frequently across all transactions.
- High unit price combined with consistent demand makes it the top revenue-generating product.
- Stocked and sold at all three branches.

### 7. Top Category — Beverages

- Beverages category (Cheese, Milk, Tea, Coffee, Juice) accounts for approximately 36% of all transactions.
- Highest revenue-generating category across all branches.
- Category includes both premium items (Cheese, Coffee) and everyday items (Milk, Tea).

### 8. Top Branch — Branch C

- Branch C (Mumbai) records the highest transaction volume and sales revenue.
- Marginally ahead of Branch A (Delhi) and Branch B (Bangalore).
- Mumbai's larger consumer base contributes to Branch C's leadership.

### 9. Top City — Mumbai

- Mumbai, served by Branch C, is the highest-performing city.
- Consistent with Branch C being the top branch.

### 10. Most Used Payment Method — UPI

- UPI is used in approximately 38% of all transactions.
- Reflects India's growing adoption of digital payment infrastructure.
- Cash (33%) and Credit Card (29%) follow in usage share.
