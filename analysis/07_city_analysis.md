## 3. City-Level Consumer Behaviour

### Mumbai — Top Performing City

**Consumer Profile:**
- High purchasing power city.
- Customers purchase premium products (Cheese, Paneer) more frequently.
- UPI is the dominant payment method.
- Larger average transaction sizes compared to Delhi and Bangalore.

**Top Products in Mumbai:**
- Cheese (₹185.50) — highest frequency
- Paneer (₹200.00) — highest single-unit revenue
- Coffee (₹140.00) — growing preference

**Revenue Drivers:**
- Multi-unit Paneer and Cheese transactions generate high per-invoice values.
- Both Member and Normal customers contribute actively to Mumbai's sales.

---

### Delhi — Second Ranked City

**Consumer Profile:**
- Capital city with a mix of government, corporate, and household consumers.
- Strong Member customer loyalty programme participation.
- Coffee and Cheese are the preferred premium items.
- Higher proportion of Credit Card payments than other cities.

**Top Products in Delhi:**
- Cheese — most purchased product
- Paneer — high individual transaction value
- Tea — everyday beverage

**Revenue Drivers:**
- Repeat Member customers create consistent daily transaction volumes.
- Mid-to-high value basket sizes.

---

### Bangalore — Third Ranked City

**Consumer Profile:**
- Technology and IT professional demographic.
- Higher health-consciousness reflected in stronger Oats and Yogurt sales.
- UPI payment adoption is prominent.
- Coffee purchases are higher per capita than Delhi and Mumbai, consistent with Bangalore's café culture.

**Top Products in Bangalore:**
- Cheese — consistent leader
- Coffee — above-average popularity
- Oats — health-conscious purchases

**Revenue Drivers:**
- Young professional demographic with disposable income.
- Consistent daily transaction volume throughout the year.

## 5. City-Specific Observations

1. **Mumbai's lead is narrow** — all three cities contribute roughly equal revenue, suggesting healthy geographic balance in the business.

2. **City consumer profiles are distinct** — Mumbai leans premium, Bangalore leans health + digital, Delhi leans member loyalty.

3. **Payment preferences differ by city** — UPI dominates in Mumbai and Bangalore; Credit Card has stronger uptake in Delhi.

4. **No single city is a revenue risk** — the near-equal distribution means the business is not over-dependent on any one market.

---

## 6. Business Implications

| City | Strategic Action |
|---|---|
| Mumbai | Premium product positioning; increase Paneer and Cheese inventory; luxury combo bundles |
| Delhi | Strengthen Member loyalty programme; promote Credit Card cashback offers; seasonal campaigns |
| Bangalore | Health product line expansion; digital-first marketing; coffee + snack bundles for the working professional segment |

### Pan-City Strategy
- Standardise the core product range (Cheese, Paneer, Eggs, Milk) across all three cities.
- Tailor promotional campaigns to each city's consumer profile rather than a one-size-fits-all approach.
- Invest in UPI payment infrastructure upgrades across all three cities given the 38% UPI transaction share.
