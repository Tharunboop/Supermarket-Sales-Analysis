## 3. Category Deep Dive

### Beverages (36% of Transactions)

**Why Beverages leads:**
- Contains the top product (Cheese at ₹185.50) and second-highest priced individual item.
- Coffee (₹140) and Juice (₹110) are mid-to-high revenue items.
- Milk and Tea are daily essentials that generate consistent transaction frequency.
- The mix of premium and everyday beverages creates broad customer appeal.

**Revenue drivers within Beverages:**
- Cheese — highest unit price, highest frequency
- Coffee — higher unit price, regular purchases
- Juice — popular in all three branches

**Key insight:** Beverages dominates because it spans both premium items (Cheese, Coffee) and necessity items (Milk, Tea), ensuring purchases occur across all customer segments.

---

### Dairy (23% of Transactions)

**Why Dairy ranks second:**
- Paneer (₹200) is the single most expensive product in the catalogue.
- Eggs purchased at high quantities (5–6 units) per transaction.
- Butter and Yogurt are regular household staples.

**Revenue drivers within Dairy:**
- Paneer — highest unit price in entire dataset
- Butter — regular repeat purchases at ₹120
- Eggs — high-volume purchases (up to 6 units)

---

### Grains (17% of Transactions)

**Profile:**
- Rice (₹80), Wheat Flour (₹90), and Oats (₹120) form this category.
- Predominantly purchased in 3–6 unit quantities.
- Wheat Flour and Oats attract health-conscious buyers.
- Consistent demand across all months.

---

### Snacks (15% of Transactions)

**Profile:**
- Chips (₹55) and Biscuits (₹45) are impulse-buy items.
- Frequently purchased alongside higher-priced items as add-ons.
- Low unit price limits revenue contribution despite high frequency.

---

### Bakery (6% of Transactions)

**Profile:**
- Bread (₹55) is the sole product in this category.
- Essential item that drives store visits but not a revenue anchor.
- Typically purchased in 2–5 units per transaction.

---

### Grocery (3% of Transactions)

**Profile:**
- Sugar (₹50) is the sole product in this category.
- Lowest revenue-generating category.
- Functions as a supplementary basket item.

---

## 4. Category Revenue Observations

1. **Beverages generates the most revenue** primarily because Cheese and Coffee carry high unit prices and are purchased frequently.

2. **Dairy is a strong second** driven entirely by the high price of Paneer and the volume purchasing pattern for Eggs.

3. **Grains, Snacks, and Bakery** serve different roles: Grains provide steady mid-range revenue, Snacks drive foot traffic and impulse purchases, Bakery brings daily essentials buyers.

4. **Grocery (Sugar)** is the weakest revenue category but important for basket completeness — customers buying Sugar often also purchase Beverages or Grains in the same visit.

---

## 5. Business Implications

| Category | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Beverages | Maintain premium product availability (Cheese, Coffee); consider seasonal promotions |
| Dairy | Ensure Paneer supply chain stability; Eggs are a volume anchor — avoid stockouts |
| Grains | Bundle Oats with Dairy or Beverages for health-focused combo offers |
| Snacks | Use as checkout counter promotions to increase basket size |
| Bakery | Explore expanding Bakery range beyond Bread to increase category share |
| Grocery | Cross-sell Sugar with Tea or Coffee as natural pairings |
